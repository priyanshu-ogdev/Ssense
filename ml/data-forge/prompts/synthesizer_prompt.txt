=========================================
GENERATION MECHANICS (TRAPS VS. SAFE-HARBORS)
=========================================
► IF GENERATING A TRAP (FORMAT B):
RULE 1 (AFFIRMATIVE ACTION): The trap sentence MUST declare a positive, irreversible corporate action. You cannot violate the law via "omission" or stating what the company "fails to do".
RULE 2 (UNCONDITIONAL ILLEGALITY): The trap sentence must use at least one absolute term (permanently, always, unconditionally, irrevocably) that makes the action undeniable.
RULE 3 (ZERO INTERNAL PERIODS - CRITICAL): The trap sentence MUST contain exactly ONE period, located as the very final character. ZERO internal periods anywhere inside the sentence (spell out Pvt, Dr, e.g.).
RULE 4 (NO RATIONALE INSIDE TRAP): The trap sentence must be a pure declaration of the illegal practice. All justifications ("Sweet Pill" camouflage) MUST go in the surrounding sentences of the paragraph, NEVER inside the trap sentence.

► IF GENERATING A COMPLIANT SECTION (FORMAT A):
- SAFE HARBOR LEXICON: Explicitly tether all data processing to "explicit, informed consent," "clear user opt-in," or "strict legal necessity."
- CLEAN GRIEVANCE MANDATE (ANTI-POISON): Grievance redressal and consent withdrawal MUST be described as free, easy, and accessible. YOU ARE STRICTLY FORBIDDEN from adding processing fees (e.g., 5,000 INR), physical notarization demands, or disincentives to compliant sections.
- SPATIAL SEPARATION: Compliant concepts MUST be placed in entirely different XML `<section>` blocks from any trap sections.
